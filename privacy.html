About this policy

This policy describes how TillFocus, published by Thanvir Jalal, uses information for its Windows focus timer. You can contact the publisher at thanvirjalal@gmail.com. Effective date: 12 November 2026. TillFocus does not require a separate TillFocus account. Microsoft Store manages Store purchases and purchase restoration.

Information stored by the app

TillFocus stores information in this Windows app's local storage so your settings and activity can be available when you reopen it:

  • Timer and break preferences, daily goal, appearance, sound choice and floating timer position.
  • Your current timer, planned finish time, pending timer choices and optional session or plan intentions.
  • Ended focus sessions, including identifiers, start and end times, outcomes, planned duration and counted focus time. Optional intention labels and their timing/status can be retained with a session.
  • Ended Focus Until plans with intentions, including plan times, intention labels and completion states.
  • Daily progress totals and named timer presets that you choose to save.
  • First-use and last-observed trial timestamps, and whether you dismissed the first-use guidance.

Intention labels and preset names can contain personal information you choose to enter. Avoid adding information you do not want stored on your computer. Counted focus time excludes paused time and time while the computer is off; it is not a measurement of the quality of your work.

How this information is used

Local information supports the timer, reopening a saved session, intentions, progress/history, reports, presets and the local premium trial. TillFocus does not send focus history or intention labels to a publisher-operated service and does not include custom activity analytics or advertising. This does not describe all processing by Windows, Microsoft Store or other services you use.

Microsoft Store and Windows

TillFocus asks Microsoft Store for product and localized price information, purchase operations and license verification. Store purchase restoration concerns premium access, not synchronization or recovery of local history. TillFocus does not collect payment-card details through its own payment form or store a publisher-managed paid-access flag in local history/trial files.

Windows and Microsoft Store can process account, purchase, device, diagnostic or other service information under their own terms and settings. This policy does not promise that those services collect no diagnostics. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for Microsoft's practices.

TillFocus uses Windows notifications and local audio for timer events. Its notification messages describe timer/plan events rather than including intention labels. Notification visibility and any retained Windows notification history depend on Windows settings and behavior.

CSV files you choose to export

When premium access is available, you can export retained ended sessions for a selected range of local end dates. The CSV contains session identifiers, timestamps, outcomes, planned duration, counted focus seconds and whether the session belonged to Focus Until. Intention labels are excluded unless you explicitly choose to include them. That option resets after an export attempt and when you open the form again. Separate plan-history records and plan intention labels are not exported.

You choose where to save the file. A shared or cloud-synchronized folder can make the CSV available to other people or transmit it through another service under that service's settings. Even without intention labels, a CSV reveals activity times and durations. TillFocus does not delete an exported file when premium access ends. The app writes a temporary file beside the chosen destination before replacing the output; if cleanup is denied, a temporary copy may remain there.

Retention and your controls

The available session history retains records ending on or after the local date 400 days before today, including that date, and at most 5,000 sessions. Plan history uses the same inclusive cutoff and at most 1,000 ended plans. Older records can become unavailable sooner when these record limits are reached. Daily progress prunes older dates when recording completion. These are application retention rules, not a guarantee that every disk copy is erased at a particular time. Reading a file can prune the in-memory history before a later successful save replaces the disk file.

Settings, current-session state, presets, trial timing and dismissed-guidance state have no fixed age-based deletion in the app. Invalid or unsupported saved files can be preserved rather than overwritten. If saving fails, the prior saved file may remain and recent activity may exist only while the app is open. Quitting, a crash or a power loss can lose unsaved activity.

You can choose whether to enter intentions, change preferences, delete individual saved presets when premium access permits, and decide what to export and share. There is currently no in-app action that erases all history or all app data. Resetting the timer is not a whole-data deletion command. App data, exported files, temporary copies, device backups and synchronized copies may need separate removal. Do not rely on purchase restoration to recover deleted local history.

Storage security

TillFocus protects your current timer and intentions, session and plan history, daily progress, saved presets, trial timing and first-use guidance state using Windows encryption tied to your Windows user/profile. Basic duration, theme, sound and floating-timer position preferences remain in Windows app settings and are not encrypted by this app protection. It does not ask for a TillFocus encryption password. Protection depends on the relevant Windows user/profile context; profile loss or moving files to another environment can prevent recovery. It is not app-exclusive protection against unrestricted software running as the same Windows user. Old plaintext copies may remain if migration cleanup is denied, and the app warns about incomplete cleanup. No secure-erasure guarantee is made.

CSV files you choose to export remain readable plaintext. Windows device protection, OS backups and any cloud/shared-folder service are separate. Do not treat CSV or local app storage as a password vault. The Windows local app-data folder can be eligible for Windows app-data backup where supported/configured; this is not a claim that backup is active on your device or a promise that a copied protected record can be restored elsewhere.
