Contact

Thanvir Jalal handles TillFocus support through thanvirjalal@gmail.com. Include your app and Windows versions and a description of the issue. Screenshots are optional and should be redacted. Focus-history files and purchase credentials are not routinely requested. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or authentication codes.

Resolved support threads and attachments will be permanently deleted from the publisher’s support mailbox within 90 days after resolution. Gmail processes support messages under Google’s Privacy Policy. This deletion commitment does not control copies held by senders or provider backup and retention systems, and does not promise secure erasure of every copy. No response-time commitment is made. See the privacy policy for information about support and hosting.

Protect your saved information

If a save warning appears, keep TillFocus open and follow the available retry guidance. Closing, a crash or power loss can lose unsaved activity. Purchase restoration does not restore local history. Avoid resetting or uninstalling as a first troubleshooting step when you need your records.

Protected local data

Saved app records depend on your Windows user/profile context. Losing that profile or moving files to another environment can prevent recovery. Do not treat copied app files as a guaranteed backup. Exports remain readable CSV files; check their contents and destination before sharing.
